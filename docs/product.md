Keeping Stock Levels Accurate</strong></h1><p>Stock changes every day as products are sold and deliveries arrive. StockSense AI handles this through three complementary mechanisms that work together:</p><h2><strong>7.1  Automatic Daily Simulation (Hands-Free)</strong></h2><p>Every night at midnight, the system automatically estimates the day's sales. For every product that was not manually updated in the past 24 hours, it subtracts the average daily sales from the current stock. This keeps stock levels approximately accurate without the owner needing to do anything.</p><table><tr><td><p><em>This is directional accuracy, not perfect accuracy. For a kirana store, knowing you have roughly 12 milk packets left (instead of exactly 11) is far more useful than stale data from three days ago showing 25. And it requires zero effort.</em></p></td></tr></table><p>As real sales data accumulates in the system (via the Record Sale or Quick Update features), the system automatically recalculates average daily sales as a rolling 7-day average from actual transactions. The longer the system runs, the more accurate its estimates become.</p><h2><strong>7.2  Quick Stock Update (5 Minutes Every Morning)</strong></h2><p>For owners who want precise data, the Quick Stock Update screen is a dedicated fast-entry interface. It shows only the products that matter — those with low stock or recent movement. Next to each product is a single number input. Enter today's actual count for each, tap Save All. Done.</p><p>This is the screen the store helper opens every morning. It is intentionally simple — no navigation, no menus, just products and numbers. Designed for someone who has never used software before.</p><h2><strong>7.3  Record Delivery (When Supplier Arrives)</strong></h2><p>When a supplier delivers goods, the owner opens the Record Delivery screen. It shows all products with pending reorder suggestions for that supplier. For each delivered item, enter the quantity received and tap Confirm. The system immediately:</p><ul><li>Increases the current stock level for each product</li><li>Updates the Last Restocked date</li><li>Changes the reorder suggestion status from Pending to Fulfilled</li><li>Resolves the active stock alert for that product if stock is now above threshold</li><li>Logs the delivery as a RESTOCK transaction in the audit trail</li><li>Updates the supplier's on-time delivery record (for future reliability score refinement)</li></ul><h1><strong>8.
